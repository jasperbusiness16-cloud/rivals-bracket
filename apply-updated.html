Requirements and Availability

Confirm every tournament requirement. Discord membership, a valid permanent Discord User ID, and tournament-day check-in are mandatory.

Discord Membership and User ID Are Required

Discord is required for team assignments, private voice channels, tournament functions, check-in, lobby instructions, substitutions, and urgent staff communication. Applicants who are not in the official Discord or do not provide their correct permanent numeric Discord User ID will not be considered.
