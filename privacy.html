Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rivals Gauntlet collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website, account features, tournament systems, predictions, community tools, and related services.

1. Who We Are

Rivals Gauntlet is an independently operated community tournament platform based in Wyoming, United States. References to “Rivals Gauntlet,” “we,” “us,” or “our” in this policy refer to the individual operator of rivalsgauntlet.com.

Questions about this policy may be sent to rivalsgauntlet@gmail.com .

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Email address and account authentication information.
  • Display name, in-game name, Discord username or Discord ID.
  • Platform, region, availability, competitive history, rank, and role preferences.
  • Biography and optional social media links.
  • Tournament applications, roster information, substitutions, and participation records.
  • Predictions, bracket selections, scores, rankings, rewards, and RG Point activity.
  • Messages, reports, support requests, and other communications you send to us.
  • Contribution or donation records that are provided to us by a payment platform, such as the contributor name, contribution amount, date, and message.

Information Collected Automatically

Our hosting and technology providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, timestamps, authentication events, error logs, and activity needed to operate and secure the service.

Public Information

Some information is intended to be displayed publicly, including display names, tournament participation, team assignments, match results, leaderboard placement, prediction statistics, championships, and optional social links.

Do not submit information to a public profile or tournament feature that you do not want others to see.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts.
  • Process tournament applications and manage event participation.
  • Assign teams, tournament roles, substitutes, and private Discord access.
  • Operate rankings, predictions, rewards, RG Points, and account statistics.
  • Display public profiles, brackets, results, leaderboards, and championship records.
  • Communicate about applications, matches, account activity, support, and platform updates.
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, account sharing, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain security, and improve platform performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service and Tournament Rules.

4. Firebase and Service Providers

Rivals Gauntlet uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database, to support account authentication, data storage, live platform information, and other site functionality.

We may also use hosting, email, payment, donation, social, or communication providers when necessary to operate a feature. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Current analytics Rivals Gauntlet does not currently state that it uses a separate advertising network or standalone analytics platform. If that changes, this policy should be updated.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With technology providers that help operate the platform.
  • With tournament staff, moderators, broadcasters, or event administrators when needed to run an event.
  • With Discord or similar services when you request or use connected tournament features.
  • Publicly, when information is part of a public profile, leaderboard, bracket, roster, result, broadcast, or championship archive.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce our rules, investigate misconduct, or secure the platform.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not use personal information for targeted advertising at this time.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the platform, maintain tournament and championship records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse, and satisfy legal or accounting requirements.

Tournament results, championships, public match records, and moderation records may be retained after an account is closed when necessary to preserve competitive history or platform integrity.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may update certain account and profile information through your account settings. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some information may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, tournament integrity, or permanent competitive records.

Send privacy requests to rivalsgauntlet@gmail.com .

8. Age Requirements and Minors

Rivals Gauntlet is not intended for children under 13, and users under 13 may not create an account or submit personal information.

Users who are at least 13 but under 18 may use the platform only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. A parent or legal guardian may also be required to verify eligibility, accept tournament documents, receive or claim any prize or reward, and provide payment or tax information where applicable.

If we learn that personal information was collected from a child under 13 without legally valid parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, its features, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

When a change is material, we may also provide notice through the website, account interface, or email when appropriate.
