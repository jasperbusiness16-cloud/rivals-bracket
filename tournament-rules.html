Official Competition Handbook

Tournament Rules

These rules apply to Rivals Gauntlet tournaments unless an individual event publishes additional or replacement rules.

1. Eligibility

  • Tournaments may be open to multiple regions unless an event specifies otherwise.
  • Current events use individual player applications. Future events may use different formats.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee selection.
  • Administrators may verify player information before accepting an application.

2. Check-In

  • Selected player check-in normally opens 1 hour before the event.
  • Selected player check-in normally closes 30 minutes before the event.
  • Substitute check-in normally opens 6 hours before the event.
  • Substitute check-in normally remains open until 24 hours after the official tournament start time.
  • Check-in windows may change for future events.

3. Discord Requirements

Participation in the official Rivals Gauntlet Discord server is required for selected players. Players must join and remain in their assigned team voice channel whenever requested by tournament staff. Failure to do so may result in substitution, forfeiture, or removal from the event.

4. Player Availability & Substitutions

  • Players are expected to remain reasonably available throughout the event.
  • Tournament administrators may substitute players whenever reasonably necessary.
  • Players may notify tournament staff if a substitute is needed.
  • Teams may be required to wait approximately 10 minutes before a substitute or forfeit decision, subject to administrator discretion.

5. Match Procedures

  • Follow tournament administrator instructions at all times.
  • Be ready when your match is called.
  • Report results using the official process for the event.
  • Event-specific procedures may replace these general procedures.

6. Disconnects & Technical Issues

Players should immediately notify tournament staff if a technical issue occurs. Where the game supports it, an in-game pause may be used. Tournament administrators may authorize additional pauses and will determine the appropriate resolution based on the circumstances.

7. Competitive Integrity

  • No cheating, hacks, unauthorized software, macros, or exploits.
  • No account sharing, boosting, smurfing, or ineligible players.
  • No match fixing, collusion, stream sniping, or intentionally misleading tournament staff.
  • Good sportsmanship is expected at all times.

8. Broadcasts

Any tournament match may be livestreamed, recorded, clipped, rebroadcast, or used in promotional material. By participating, players acknowledge that their gameplay, in-game name, and tournament participation may appear in Rivals Gauntlet content.

9. Prizes

  • Prize distribution is generally targeted within 30 days after the event.
  • Payment methods will be announced for each event (such as PayPal or another designated method).
  • Prize recipients may be required to verify identity and eligibility.
  • Eligible minors may be required to have a parent or legal guardian receive prizes on their behalf.

10. Appeals

Players may submit one appeal within 24 hours of a tournament decision and should include relevant evidence. Filing an appeal does not automatically pause the tournament or reverse an earlier decision.

11. Administrator Authority

Tournament administrators may make reasonable rulings for situations not expressly covered by these rules in order to preserve fairness, competitive integrity, and the operation of the event.

12. Event-Specific Rules

These Tournament Rules are the general rules for Rivals Gauntlet events. Individual tournaments may publish additional or replacement rules. When a conflict exists, the event-specific rules govern that event.
